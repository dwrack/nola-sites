Two Sisters Soul Food in Treme

Two Sisters sits on Basin Street at the Tremé edge, in a low building with SOUL FOOD painted down the side of it in red. Inside is a proper dining room rather than a takeout counter: yellow checkered cloths, red walls, chairs pulled up to tables set for people who intend to sit a while. Somebody comes and takes the order at the table.

The menu is short and it is the real thing. Smothered turkey wings and necks, stewed hen, stuffed bell peppers, fried catfish, BBQ chicken, and gumbo that comes with potato salad in it. Stewed oxtails and chitterlings cost seven dollars more than everything else and are the two dishes people arrange their week around. Every plate comes with rice and gravy and vegetables, which is printed at the bottom of the menu in case anybody was unsure.

Good to know

  • Dine in at the table, takeout, or delivery
  • Every plate comes with rice and gravy and vegetables
  • Black-owned, per the Google profile
  • Catering available
  • Free lot out front and free street parking around it
