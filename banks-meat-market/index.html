Banks Meat Market

Banks Meat Market anchors the corner of Broad and Banks, and everyone within ten blocks has a Banks order. The butcher case in back runs deep: fresh cuts, smoked sausage by the pound, hot sausage patties, turkey necks, whatever the week's plate needs. The hot counter up front turns it into lunch.

The po'boys are the draw. Roast beef with gravy, hot sausage, fried shrimp, fried chicken, and the famous 32-inch that gets ordered for job sites, tailgates, and repasts. Reviewers call it the best neighborhood corner store in Mid-City, bar none. It is also where you cash a check, send a Western Union, and pick up the beer.

Good to know
