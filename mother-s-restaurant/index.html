About

Popular cafeteria-style joint drawing crowds with po' boys & down-home Southern fare since 1938. Mother's Restaurant is a po’ boys restaurant in Central Business District, known for jambalaya, cheese grits, red beans and rice, etouffee and shrimp creole.
