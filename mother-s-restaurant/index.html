Mother's Restaurant

Simon and Mary Landry opened Mother's on Poydras Street in 1938 to feed the longshoremen and newspapermen of the old warehouse district. The Marines adopted it during the war, the sign still says World's Best Baked Ham, and the line still forms before the doors open. Eighty-eight years in, it is one of the last places downtown that runs exactly the way it always has.

You order at the counter, take a number, and find a seat under the photos. The Ferdi Special, baked ham and roast beef with debris and gravy, is the sandwich the city is known for. Breakfast is served all day: biscuits, cheese grits, eggs, and that ham. Then jambalaya, red beans, etouffee, shrimp Creole, fried chicken, turnip greens, bread pudding.
