Small Mart Cafe

Small Mart is the corner store idea done properly: a white Creole building on Chartres with a balcony over the sidewalk and a round sign hanging off the corner. Inside there is a pastry case, a rack of bagels and croissants, a chalkboard, and string lights over a handful of tables. Doors at eight, closed Mondays, which is about the only thing anybody complains about.

The kitchen is where the ninety percent five-star rating comes from. Vegan is not a token section of the menu here, it is most of the reason people write reviews at all: tofu with avocado and vegan cream cheese on an everything bagel, a vegan breakfast sandwich more than one person calls the best they have ever eaten, sandwiches packed with pickled carrot and greens. The coffee arrives with art on it. Reviewers bring up the owner by way of explaining why the room feels good to sit in.

Good to know

  • Open 8am Tuesday to Sunday, closed Mondays
  • Bagels, croissants, donuts and pastries from open
  • Vegan options right across the menu, not a side section
  • Espresso drinks, drip coffee, juice and lemonade
  • Tables inside under the string lights, or take it out

What to order

The regulars' list

No. 01

Everything Bagel, Loaded

Avocado, tofu, vegan cream cheese. Reviewers use the word filling, which for a bagel is exactly the point.

No. 02

The Loaded Sandwich

Pickled carrot, greens and something fried, wrapped in paper and handed across. Two hands required.

No. 03

Latte

Poured with art on top, in a Small Mart cup. Order one with whatever you are eating and take a table.

No. 04

Straight Off the Rack

Bagels and croissants stacked up before eight. On a weekend get there early, because the doors shut at 1:30.
