The Upper Quarter

The sign hanging off the corner at Bienville says THE UPPER QUARTER, and underneath that, A NEIGHBORHOOD BAR. In this part of the French Quarter that is a statement of intent. The building is the classic one: brick, wrought iron, a balcony over the sidewalk, and a courtyard out back hung with beads and whatever is growing.

The room is small, which reviewers keep listing as a virtue rather than a complaint. Bartenders make gin cocktails and have actual beer opinions, prices come up again and again as fair, and there is a chalkboard by the door that reads POP-UP ALERT when somebody is cooking that night. In season they boil crawfish and put the trays straight down on the table. Visitors keep writing that they would be regulars if they lived here.

Good to know

  • Open noon to 2am, seven days a week
  • Courtyard out back off the Bienville corner
  • Full bar, gin cocktails, bartenders who will actually recommend a beer
  • Food pop-ups some nights, crawfish boils in season
  • French Quarter prices without the Bourbon Street markup

The regulars' list

A Shot and a High Life

The order that tells the bartender you are staying a while. Set down together, no comment.

Boiled Crawfish, In Season

By the tray, poured out on the table with corn in the pile. Bring people and roll your sleeves up.

No. 03

A Gin Cocktail, Bartender's Pick

Reviewers single out the gin drinks and the beer recommendations. Say roughly what you like and let them work.

Whatever the Pop-Up Is Cooking

The chalkboard by the door says POP-UP ALERT when there is food that night. Check it on the way in.
